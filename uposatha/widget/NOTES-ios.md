## Behaviour (decisions)
- Layers per spec: 1 Uposatha+kala (-> tab home), 2 Day & night (-> parts), 3 Month (-> cal). Month cells in medium/large are `Link`s to `tab=cal&day=<ymd>`; small = one target. Lock screen: rectangular 2 layers (vertical dots), circular 3 (tap on the circle: counter -> kala ring -> date), inline one line.
- (superseded by the owner round below: segments + chevrons, left/right halves) Dots = one `Button(intent: SwitchLayerIntent)` over the whole bottom strip (iOS 17+, `#available`). **Layer state is per widget SIZE** (App Group key `layer.small|medium|large|accessoryRectangular|accessoryCircular`): iOS gives no per-instance id without a configuration, so two widgets of one size share a layer. iOS 15/16: layer 1 only, no dots.
- Timeline (24 h): now + every 30 min + dawn/aruna/noon/sunset + part borders + Uposatha start/end + the counter's hours counted back from its target (so "1 d 7 h" flips on time) + coverage end; ~50 entries; policy `.after(last)`. Layer is read once per timeline (the intent triggers a reload). Spec said hourly; 30 min keeps the "now" tick of the day scale honest.
- No data / generatedAt older than 14 days / now outside `days` (needs a following day for vikala's end) -> "Open Uposatha" (ru/en by the device language), no times. Placeholder/gallery: the bundled sample, rendered at its own `generatedAt`.
- Language = `settings.lang`. Kala = aruna..noon, vikala = noon..next aruna (WIDGET.md); "until dawn" shows the next aruna `hm`. No place (`placeSet:false`): the kala row of layer 1 is replaced by "times assumed: 18:00 and 06:00" (as in the mock-up); layers 2 and lock screen still show kala/vikala (lock screen shows "no place set" instead).
- "N min left" is `Text(date, style: .relative)` (live, system-formatted): it follows the DEVICE language, not the app's, and reads "1 hr, 34 min" style. `Text(timerInterval:)` is iOS 16 only and only prints h:mm:ss, so it was not used.
- Part borders (`parts` have only HH:MM) are placed by cumulative durations from the sunrise moment; the place's UTC offset is taken from the data's own ms/ymd/hm (no tz database). A DST change inside one night could shift the later parts' borders by an hour (display uses the app's own HH:MM, only "which part is now" would be off).
- Font: Lato exists on the box only as `.woff2` (iOS cannot load it from UIAppFonts). The widget uses the system font through one function, `dgFont()` in `WidgetTheme.swift`; to switch bundle Lato TTFs, add them to UIAppFonts and change that function.

## Owner-test round (2026-10-10, same changes as Android)
- Data: `days` starts yesterday; the part/kala lookup takes the last day with sunrise <= now, so the night before dawn is found in `days[0]` and never falls to the placeholder. `uposathas[].day` is the Uposatha's own day. `settings.weekStart` (0 Sunday / 1 Monday; absent: ru 1, en 0) drives header names, column offsets and rows (grid and the small widget's two-week strip).
- Month grid: solid = `start.ymd`, light = `day`; one strip when `day == start+1` and both sit in one row (breaks at the row's last column, not "Sunday"; `StripShape` drops the inner corners); on a shared date solid wins and the strip runs through it (back-to-back 14th/15th both render). Cells now touch (no 2 pt gaps), 1 pt inset each.
- Labels (Next rows, month list, captions, circular date, "in N d") use `start.ymd`; `lite.from`/span already used start.
- Moons: layer-1 hero (and lock-screen moons) = `today.moon + (now - generatedAt)/29.530588853 d` mod 1 (`WSnap.heroPhase`); list rows use `WUposatha.listPhase` (15th full .5 / new 0, 8th .25 / .75, 14th .375 waxing / .875).
- Halo (dark mode, every moon incl. new, not on the mono lock screen): blurred disk #E9E4DC alpha .26+.22*illumination, blur (3+3.5*illumination)/100 of the size, plus a .8/100 rim #E9E4DC alpha .45; light mode keeps the navy rim. It is drawn outside the moon's frame, so a moon at a widget's edge may have its halo clipped.
- Layer switch: three 22x6 segments (gap 6, current `dgAccent`, others `dgBorderStrong`) between two chevrons; the strip is 44 pt high and full width, left half = `SwitchLayerIntent(step: -1)`, right half = `step: +1` (iOS 17+; before that, one layer, no switch). On small/medium text layers the strip overlaps the bottom ~18 pt of the content (a tap there switches instead of opening the app); the month layer and large widget keep clear of it (the medium month cells shrank to 13 pt high, font 11). Lock screen keeps the vertical dots / whole-circle tap (step +1). iOS 17's own 16 pt content margins plus the 44 pt strip make the medium widget tight: check it on a device.

## Tables and the large size (2026-10-10)
- `UpoTable`: every Uposatha list (layer 1 "Next", the month layer's list) is a table: moon | date it begins on | day ("8th") | right-aligned "in N d" ("now" for one that is on). Date and day columns are as wide as the widest entry (measured with `UIFont`, so rows line up; text scales down to 0.8 if a system font differs). Medium month list: short dates ("Sat 17"), no "in N d" column.
- Large, layer 1: `UpoNext` lists up to 6 rows, as many as the height left under the hero allows (GeometryReader, 25 pt per row).
- Large, layer 3: grid cells 24 pt; under it `MonthBottom`: up to 4 rows (on + next), and a `TodayBlock` (moon waxing/waning + % lit from the clock-advanced `today.moon`, dawn / noon / sunset of today) only if 54 pt are still free. On iOS 17 (44 pt switch reserve) it will rarely fit: expect it on a 5-row month with few Uposathas, or on iOS 16.
- Strip: a cell is square only on the side that joins its neighbour (the 14th/15th shared date joins both sides); the today ring is a capsule inset 2 pt.

## Risks / not verified
- Never compiled. Likely trouble spots if CI fails: `Text + Text` chains (KalaRow, Caption, Counter), `@available` guards in ViewBuilders (DotsStrip, LockScreenView), the AppIntent (`@Parameter` + `init()`), the pbxproj hand edit. The checker proves graph/brace/key consistency, not types.
- iOS 18 tinted / accented rendering is not designed (colours become one tint; kala vs vikala differ by the word, as the spec says). The moon image is not marked for accented mode.
- A `reloadAllTimelines()` call per `put` (the bridge debounces and only sends changed JSON).
- The page must understand `&day=` on `/uposatha-calendar` (the bridge reads only `tab=`); `w=` is ignored by it. Tap on the "no place" note opens Home, not the place setting (no known route).
- Dots' tap area is the strip (full width x 32 pt), not a full third of the widget.

### What was built

Lock screen: rectangular / circular / inline.
  - `UposathaMoonWidget`: `systemSmall` = the moon and "30% ↑" (`moonOnly()`), `accessoryCircular` = the flat sign over "30%".
  - `UposathaDayWidget`: `systemSmall` = Day & night compact (`dayNight()`, the narrow branch).
  - Names / descriptions en + ru by the device language (`WidgetNames`).
- **One wording table** for every size: `extension WSnap` in `WidgetModel.swift` (keys `w.*`): title "15th day · coming / now", counter "1d 7h", date + "from 18:00" / "until 18:00", the moon NOW with one of 8 names + percent, "Kala until 12:41" (green) / "Vikala until 05:50" (red), details = one more line. The kala line follows `settings.showKala` (the Day & night design always has it). The old "no place set / times assumed" replacement of the kala row is gone, as on Android.
- **Scaling** (`WidgetViews.swift`): every design is written in Android's base numbers; `GeometryReader` gives the widget's size; lines are measured with `UIFont` (Lato) through `measuredWidth`; needW / needH are summed as in `WidgetViews.java`; `k = WPlan.scale(...)` (`WM.fit` adds the same 3 % of spare width); every font size, gap, padding and the moon are multiplied by k. Texts are `WLine`: one line, `fixedSize()`, a frame exactly 1.2 em high (Lato's ascent + descent), so nothing is cut with "…" and the heights are the ones the plan counted. `WPlan` (in `WidgetModel.swift`) is the port of `WidgetPlan.java`.
- **Paddings**: the designs carry their own (12-16 base points x k), so iOS 17's content margins are switched off (`dgNoMargins()` -> `contentMarginsDisabled()`); before iOS 17 the view already fills the widget.
- **Lato** without a project change: `Assets.xcassets/lato_{regular,semibold,bold}.dataset` (the same TTFs as Android's `res/font`), loaded with `NSDataAsset`, registered once with `CTFontManagerRegisterGraphicsFont` (`DgFonts.ready` in `WidgetTheme.swift`). PostScript names (read from the files' name tables, `fc-scan` agrees): `Lato-Regular`, `Lato-Semibold`, `Lato-Bold`. `dgFont()` = `Font.custom(name, fixedSize:)` (fixed, no Dynamic Type: the design is measured and scaled), `dgUIFont()` / `measuredWidth()` use the same face; if the registration failed both fall back to the system font together.
- **Colours**: Android's table (`WidgetConfig.LIGHT_C / DARK_C`, `gen_android_res.py` PALETTE): surface #EEF4F2 / #1A211F, tile, band, gold frame of today, plate, part colours of the day bar.
- **Moon** (`MoonView.swift`): earthshine instead of a black disc (shade #1F2A3C light / #1B2535 dark; penumbra 0.46 under a sharp edge 0.70, 0.60 under 50 pt), rims and the dark-theme halo that fades to nothing at the frame's edge, as in `WidgetMoon.java`. `disc` is the disc's diameter; the big moon's frame is disc / 0.88 (the halo's room), rows under it are inset as on Android. `mini: true` = list rows and calendar cells: neutral rim, no halo; under 24 pt one flat shade (0.78) instead of two blurs (a calendar can have 30 of them). `MoonGlyph` = the flat sign of the lock screen (the mock-up's `glyph()`).
- **Slides**: `SwitchLayerIntent` as before (iOS 17+, per size in the App Group, left half back / right half forward). Nothing stored: medium 0, large 2 (`WStore.layer`). Slides per size: `WidgetFamilies.layerCount` (medium 2, large 3); the medium's switch has two segments and turns 0 <-> 1, a stored slide the size does not have (an old 2 on the medium) shows the first. iOS 15/16: that default slide, no switch. The switch is drawn at the very bottom, in the designs' own bottom padding; it takes only 10 pt from the designs (`SlidesView.reserve`), its tap strip is 34 pt high.
- **Calendar**: tiles, today in the gold frame, the evening an Uposatha begins on = number in a filled circle, its own day = a light band, mini-moons under Uposatha days and, with `settings.allMoons`, under every day (`moons.phase`, fallback: today's moon carried at 29.530588853 d per cycle, `WSnap.phaseOn`), the card of today, "Next" when the height is left. Every day is a `Link` to `tab=cal&day=`. The month arrows (iOS 17+) reuse `SwitchLayerIntent` with the key `month` (0..2 ahead); the choice is forgotten on the next day of the place (`WStore.monthOffset`, stamp `at.month`).
- **Data**: `settings.allMoons`, top-level `moons { from, phase[] }` (both optional). The current Uposatha is the first whose END is after now (`WSnap`, `WData.current(at:)`), never an index. `widget-sample.json` = `widget/fixture-ru.json`; the gallery shows it in the device's language (`WStore.sample`).
- **Timeline**: a stamp every 15 minutes now (was 30), as Android's refresh: "N min left" and the "now" mark of the day bar are static texts / shapes, so they are as fresh as the last entry.
- **Lock screen**: one scenario each (no layers, no dots): rectangular = sign + "8th day · coming" / "1d 7h" / "Kala until 12:41"; circular = sign over "1d 7h"; inline = an SF Symbol of the phase + "8th day · 1d 7h · Kala until 12:41". There a text may shrink (`minimumScaleFactor`), it is never cut.
- Tools: `gen_ios_strings.py` already carried the `w.*` keys and the two arrays (`w.weekdaysLong`, `w.monthsOf` land in `WidgetStrings.lists`); one extra key added, `unit.min`. `check_ios_widget.py` unchanged.

### Where iOS differs from Android on purpose
- 2x2 arrangement (`WPlan.upoArrangement`): Android falls to the two-line stack whenever the one-line stack does not fit at full size (its cells are narrow and tall). An iPhone's small widget is square: there the one-line stack fails by height and the two-line one would be smaller still, so of the two the bigger wins (158 pt with details: 0.97 instead of 0.84). The small design may go down to 0.6 (Android 0.75), so the 141 pt widget of the smallest phones is not cut.
- Calendar scale (`WPlan.calScale`): Android scales by the width (its 4x4 is 298x441). An iPhone's large is 338x354: at that scale the weeks would be too low for the mini-moons. So the scale is the one at which mini-moons + the card of today fit (as the approved sheet 09-ios shows), when that is at least 0.9 (`calWantScale`); else the month alone, down to 0.55 (a fallback only: the medium no longer shows the calendar).
- The switch takes 10 pt, not Android's 22: it overlaps the bottom padding.
- No per-widget settings, no "Moon + Uposatha 2x1" and "4x1 strip" (iOS has no such sizes).

### Numbers expected (the plan's maths run with Lato's own metrics, not on a device)
small 158: stacked, k 1.08 (0.97 with details); 141: 0.97 / 0.87; 170: 1.16 / 1.04. Medium 338x158: Uposatha 1.11 (0.98 with details), Day & night 1.2. Large 338x354: Uposatha tall, k 1.2, 2-3 "Next" rows; Day & night tall 1.2; Calendar 1.10 with card + mini-moons (a six-week month 1.00). (The medium has no calendar slide: it would be 0.62-0.70.)

### Not verified / likely trouble on the first build
- `dgNoMargins()` (`UposathaWidget.swift`): `some WidgetConfiguration` with two different underlying types, one under `if #available` (SE-0360, Swift 5.7+; Apple's docs list `contentMarginsDisabled()` as iOS 15+, in which case the guard is simply not needed). If the compiler refuses: delete the helper and its three calls; the designs then sit inside the system margins (double padding, smaller k), nothing else breaks.
- Font registration: `CTFontManagerRegisterGraphicsFont` is deprecated from iOS 18 (a warning at most with a 15.0 target). If `DgFonts.ready` is false on a device everything is drawn in the system font (still measured consistently).
- `@available(iOS 17.0, *)` helpers inside views (`LayerSwitch.stepButton`, `MonthNav.arrow`), `Button(intent:)`.
- Baseline rows (`WCounter`, `WKalaLine`, `WPercent`): `HStack(alignment: .firstTextBaseline)` of texts with paddings, then `fixedSize().frame(height:)`.
- `Link` around a flexible calendar tile (`CalendarDesign.cell`): the 7 tiles of a week must share the width equally; if a `Link` hugs its label instead, the grid collapses to the numbers' widths.
- Blur and the dynamic `UIColor` in a widget's archived view (v1 had both, also never run). The halo is a radial gradient now, not a blur.
- The SF Symbol names of the inline lock widget (`moonphase.*`, iOS 16): a wrong name shows no icon, nothing worse.
- iOS 18 tinted home screen: not designed (the photo moon turns into a blob there; `MoonGlyph` could replace it under `widgetRenderingMode == .accented`, guarded for iOS 16+/18).
- Tap on a moon widget opens tab home; Android also adds `&moon=1` (the moon plays its cycle once). `DgShortcutsPlugin.deliver(url:)` does not pass it on: an app-side change, not done.

### Next step (not in this task)
Per-widget settings (theme light / dark / system, card transparency, details / kala / all-moons overrides, slides on / off): iOS needs an `AppIntentConfiguration` (iOS 17) with a `WidgetConfigurationIntent` for that, i.e. a second configuration next to the `StaticConfiguration` for iOS 15/16. It would also give every widget its own identity, so the slide and the calendar's month could be kept per widget instead of per size.
- iOS 15 devices: the extension imports `AppIntents` (iOS 16+) and the target has no `-weak_framework AppIntents` (v1 was the same). The linker normally weak-links a framework whose every used symbol is newer than the target; if the widget does not load on iOS 15 ("Library not loaded: AppIntents"), add `OTHER_LDFLAGS = ("$(inherited)", "-weak_framework", "AppIntents")` to both configurations of the widget target in `project.pbxproj` (not touched in this task; there is no way to ask for a weak link from the Swift sources, and the intent needs the import).
